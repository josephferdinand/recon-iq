# Database extension inventory

Enable required extensions in your NEW project before attempting restore. Provider-managed schemas/auth/storage are supplied by the new Supabase project. No extension is enabled remotely by this package.

- `plpgsql` in `pg_catalog`
- `pg_stat_statements` in `extensions`
- `uuid-ossp` in `extensions`
- `pgcrypto` in `extensions`
- `supabase_vault` in `vault`
- `citext` in `extensions`
- `pg_net` in `extensions`
- `pg_cron` in `pg_catalog`
- `pgmq` in `pgmq`
- `ltree` in `public`
- `vector` in `extensions`
- `pg_trgm` in `public`
- `plpgsql_check` in `extensions`
